Tool FAQs

How do I convert cm to inches?

Divide centimetres by 2.54. So a 190 cm bed length is 190 ÷ 2.54 = 74.8 inches. To go the other way, multiply inches by 2.54.

Why does UK furniture use both cm and feet/inches?

The UK went metric for manufacturing but imperial sizes stuck around in everyday speech. Retailers list metric dimensions (often with the traditional imperial name like "4'6" double") so shoppers from both habits can compare.

What is the standard UK double bed size?

A UK double bed (and the mattress to fit it) is 135 × 190 cm, traditionally called 4'6" × 6'3". Always check the retailer's exact centimetre dimensions before ordering bedding.
