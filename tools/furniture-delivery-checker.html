Furniture Delivery Checker

Free tool · No signup · Instant answer. Enter your furniture's dimensions and your narrowest doorway — we'll tell you whether it will fit through, and what to take off if it won't.

In short: a piece of furniture fits through a doorway when its two smallest dimensions fit inside the door opening — the longest side goes through first. Most UK internal doors are only 76 cm wide, so sofas usually need their legs off (and often the door off its hinges) to get through.

Use the packaged dimensions from the product listing — that's what the delivery driver brings. Width is the longest side as it stands; height is floor to top.

Measure the clear opening with the door open — not the frame, and not the door leaf. If the door can come off its hinges, tick that below.

How the check works

  • Two smallest dimensions: the item passes when its two smallest sides fit inside the door opening — the longest side goes through first, along the direction of travel.
  • 1 cm clearance: we allow 1 cm of total clearance. Anything less and you will scrape the frame, the architrave or the paint.
  • What you can take off: legs (about 10 cm of height), back cushions (about 12 cm of depth) and the door itself (about 3 cm of width) — tick the boxes and the maths updates.
  • Tight turns: as a rule of thumb, a rigid item turns a hallway corner only if its length is less than the hallway width plus the doorway width. Measure the actual turn — this part is approximate.
  • What it can't check: stair turns, radiators, light fittings and low ceilings on the route. Measure those the same way: narrowest width first, then height.

Victorian terraces can have internal doors as narrow as 70 cm, and flat entrance doors vary widely — always measure your own doorway rather than trusting the standard.

Tool FAQs

What is the standard UK internal door width?

Most UK internal doors are 76 cm (30 inches) wide in older homes, and 78 cm in many newer builds. Always measure your own doorway — Victorian terraces can be 70 cm or less, and the architrave eats a centimetre or two.

Can a sofa fit through a 76 cm door?

Most 2-seater sofas fit through a 76 cm door only with the legs off — and often with the back cushions out and the door off its hinges as well. Most 3-seater sofas will not fit through a 76 cm internal door at all and need the wider front door, or delivery in sections. Check before you order: drivers can refuse delivery and still charge.

What if my furniture will not fit through the door?

Remove the legs and back cushions, and take the door off its hinges — together that usually frees 10–15 cm. Ask the retailer about split-section or flat-pack delivery, or try the front door or patio doors as an alternative route. Never force it: scratched frames and torn fabric cost more than a re-delivery.
