Mattress Finder Quiz

Free tool · No signup · 30 seconds. Answer four quick questions about how you sleep and we'll match you to the right mattress type and firmness.

In short: side sleepers need pressure relief, back sleepers need even support, stomach sleepers need a firmer surface — and your weight and budget narrow it down to one of four types: memory foam, pocket sprung, hybrid or latex.

How the recommendation works

  • Sleep position first: it decides whether you need pressure relief (side), even support (back) or a firm, flat surface (stomach).
  • Weight adjusts firmness: heavier sleepers sink deeper, so they need a firmer feel for the same level of support.
  • Budget keeps it real: no point recommending a premium latex mattress on a £200 budget — we match the type to what you'd actually spend.
  • Four honest types: memory foam, pocket sprung, hybrid and natural latex cover the vast majority of UK mattresses.

Tool FAQs

What mattress type is best for side sleepers?

Side sleepers usually sleep best on memory foam or hybrid mattresses in a medium to medium-soft firmness. These cushion the shoulders and hips while keeping the spine aligned — a very firm pocket-sprung mattress can create pressure points for side sleepers.

How firm should my mattress be?

As a rough guide: side sleepers suit medium-soft to medium, back sleepers suit medium to medium-firm, and stomach sleepers need medium-firm to firm for proper spinal support. Heavier sleepers generally need a firmer mattress than lighter sleepers in the same position.

How much should I spend on a mattress in the UK?

Decent UK double mattresses start around £150–£200. The £200–£400 range buys a good quality memory foam or pocket-sprung mattress, while over £400 opens up premium hybrids and natural latex. This quiz factors your budget into its recommendation.
