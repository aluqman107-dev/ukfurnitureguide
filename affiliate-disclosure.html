Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Plain-English version of how this site makes money.

In short: UK Furniture Guide is reader-supported. When you buy through links on our pages, the retailer pays us a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions never influence which products we recommend or how we rank them.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a normal product link with a tracking code. If you click it and buy something, the retailer knows the sale came from us and pays us a percentage — typically 3–8% for furniture. The price you pay is exactly the same as if you'd gone to the retailer directly.

Which programmes do we use?

We currently earn affiliate commission through the Amazon UK Associates programme only (our Associate tag is ukfurnituregu-21). Links to other retailers on our pages are plain, non-affiliate links for now — we'll update this page if we join further programmes.

Does this affect our recommendations?

No — and this is the whole point of the site. Our editorial rules:

  • Products are researched and ranked on merit: quality, value, and suitability for UK homes.
  • Commission rates are never a ranking factor. A product that pays more but offers less will rank lower — or not appear at all.
  • Every recommendation lists honest cons alongside the pros.
  • We link to non-affiliate retailers too (for example, UK Furniture Online) where relevant, simply because it's useful.

What about display advertising?

In future we may show display advertisements (for example via Google AdSense). Advertisers don't influence our content either — editorial and advertising are kept strictly separate.

Why disclose this?

Because it's the law (UK consumer protection rules require clear disclosure of commercial relationships) and because it's the right thing to do. Trust is the only asset a guide site has — we'd rather over-explain than leave you guessing.
