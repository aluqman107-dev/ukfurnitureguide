# Product Content Factual Audit — ukfurnitureguide.co.uk listicles
**Audit date:** 2026-10-05
**Scope:** All 26 "Our Store Pick" cards across the 4 listicle pages, checked against the live ukfurnitureonline.co.uk product pages.
**Method:** Card data extracted from local HTML; each linked product URL fetched live via browser.

## Executive summary
- **Prices: 22 of 22 verifiable prices MATCH** the live store (the 4 category-linked cards have invented price ranges).
- **Product names: 26 of 26 are FICTIONAL.** Not a single card uses the store's real product name.
- **Guarantees: systematically inflated.** Every real product page states a **1-Year Warranty**. The guide claims 2, 3, 5, 8 and 10 years.
- **Dimensions/materials: largely invented** — wrong in most cards, sometimes describing a completely different product type.
- **4 cards use CATEGORY links** instead of product links (beds ×3, dining ×1).
- **All 6 mattress cards link to BED products**, not mattresses. The store does not appear to sell standalone mattresses at all — the entire mattresses listicle is built on invented standalone-mattress products.
- **1 card links to an EMPTY category** (bunk beds — "No Bunk Bed listings yet").
